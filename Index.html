Arcade for Clubs

Arcade is a Hack Club program where your members build games and get rewarded. Run it as a workshop at your next meeting — no experience needed, and it works for complete beginners. Clubs are welcome from anywhere in the world!

Running a workshop

Host an Arcade workshop at your club meeting. Members will build their own game, host it on GitHub Pages, and submit it. Each approved submission earns them rewards. You can use our workshop guide to run the session.

Arcade for Individuals

Building solo? No problem. Arcade is open to any teenager who wants to build a game and get rewarded for shipping it. No club needed — just you, your code, and your ideas.

How it works

Build a game, host it on GitHub Pages or itch.io, and submit it. Each approved submission earns you rewards. Check out the requirements & examples page first.

Steps

1

Build your game

2

Host it online

3

Submit your game

4

Claim your reward!

Rules & Guidelines

Requirements

  • You must be between 13 and 18 years old.
  • You must not be banned from Hackatime.
  • You must spend at least 1 hour working on your project.
  • Your project must be your own work.

Individual Submission Requirements

  • You must be a member of the Hack Club Slack.
  • Your identity must be verified using Hack Club Auth.
  • You must track your time using Hackatime.
  • You must submit a working project.

AI Usage Policy

AI can be a useful tool for learning, debugging, brainstorming, and understanding concepts.

  • AI usage should remain below 30% of your project work.
  • You must understand all code you submit.
  • Blindly copy-pasting AI-generated projects is not allowed.
  • Projects that are primarily AI-generated may be rejected.
  • Workshops with excessive AI-generated submissions may be reviewed and could risk closure.

If a reviewer asks you to explain your code, you should be able to clearly explain how it works.

Honesty Policy

Do not fabricate hours, commits, screenshots, project progress, submissions, or any other information.

False or misleading submissions may result in rejection and may affect your ability to participate in future Hack Club programs.
